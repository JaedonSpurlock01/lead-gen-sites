## Keywords intentionally not forced into the site

- **Near me:** Device location and Google Business Profile relevance determine “near me” visibility. Repeating “near me” in copy does not improve proximity.
- **Same-day, next-day, 24-hour, cheapest, or flat-rate:** The business did not supply these service guarantees.
- **Exact prices, dimensions, tonnage, or rental days:** Inventory and commercial terms were not supplied.
- **Licensed, insured, locally owned, family owned, or top-rated:** These claims were not verified.
- **Junk removal:** The site explains the difference but does not claim labor-based hauling.
- **Additional cities or county pages:** The brief limits copy and `areaServed` to Haines City.
