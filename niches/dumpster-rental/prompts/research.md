# Local research: dumpster rental in {{city}}, {{region}}

Goal: a short, sourced brief that the keyword map and every page are written from. It is the only place facts about the city enter the site, so every claim the copy makes later must trace back to a line here.

## Gather

Research the live web for {{city}}, {{regionName}}. For each item, record the fact and the URL it came from. Mark anything you could not confirm as **unverified**; unverified items stay out of the copy.

1. **Where the debris goes.** The landfill, transfer station or solid-waste department that serves the city, and what it accepts: household waste, construction and demolition debris, yard waste, concrete and dirt. County or city solid-waste pages.
2. **Restricted items.** What the local facility or state rules keep out of a roll-off (paint, tires, appliances, batteries, electronics, hazardous waste) and where residents take those instead, such as a household hazardous waste drop-off.
3. **Placement rules.** Whether a dumpster on a street, sidewalk or right-of-way needs a city permit, and any HOA or code-enforcement rules on driveway containers. Only if a source says so.
4. **Weather that affects delivery.** Rainy season and soft ground, storm or hurricane debris surges, winter freeze or snow. NOAA/NWS climate normals or the county emergency-management site.
5. **What drives projects locally.** Housing type and age, new subdivisions, roofing and remodeling demand, seasonal moves, commercial corridors. Use the city's own neighborhood names only when a source shows them.
6. **Competitor SERP.** Search `dumpster rental {{city}}`, `roll off dumpster {{city}}`, `construction dumpster {{city}}`, `dumpster sizes {{city}}`. List what the top local results emphasize, the sizes and rental terms they publish, and the services they claim. Note services we do not offer (junk removal, portable toilets, demolition) so the copy does not claim them.
7. **Keyword checks.** The `keyword-research` skill's site run adds this section; leave space for it.

## Output

Write `niches/dumpster-rental/sites/{{id}}/research.md`:

- Research date (today) and the sources list.
- A "Local context to preserve" list of 4–6 specific facts the pages should use (the disposal facility, a restricted-items rule, a placement rule, a weather pattern, a local project type).
- The competitor notes from step 6.

This niche has no `market` fields in site.json, so the brief feeds the copy only.

Done when every context bullet has a source and nothing unverified is presented as fact.
