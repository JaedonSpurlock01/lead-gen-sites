Verified local context:

- The City of Idaho Falls publishes a photovoltaic solar energy systems permit application.
- The permit form distinguishes roof-mounted and ground-mounted panels and asks for panel and roof details.
- Idaho Falls Power says customers need a city construction permit and an approved grid-connection agreement.
- Current utility materials request a one-line drawing, equipment details, system size, installer information, and electrician information.
- Idaho Falls Power performs utility review and meter-related steps for approved grid-connected systems.

Forms, fees, service policies, and rate rules can change. Published copy should direct customers to current city and utility sources instead of freezing dollar amounts or rate promises into evergreen text.
