## What driveway paving covers

Asphalt driveway paving replaces gravel, broken concrete, or tired blacktop with a smooth surface for parking and daily access. Asphalt Paving St George builds residential drives across St. George, from older streets near downtown to hillside lots where slope and runoff matter.

A driveway is a small pavement with a hard job. It carries the same car in the same wheel path every day, then takes a intense UV and triple-digit summer heat. If the base is soft or the slope traps water against the garage, the surface will crack no matter how black it looks on day one.

We price new construction, removal and replacement, and overlays. An overlay only makes sense when the existing asphalt is stable and the edges still have height against concrete, landscaping, and garage floors. If the drive has alligator cracking or pumping fines, we recommend taking it out.

Whether you call it asphalt driveway paving, blacktop driveway work, or gravel-to-asphalt conversion, the scope is the same: a compacted base and hot-mix asphalt built for southern Utah heat, UV, and storm runoff.

## Why homeowners pave with asphalt

A paved drive cuts dust, creates a clean entrance, and makes the front of the house look finished. Asphalt also allows future milling or resurfacing instead of removing a full concrete slab.

Southern Utah adds a few rules. Strong UV oxidizes unprotected asphalt, extreme surface temperatures soften tender pavement, and short cloudbursts expose poor drainage. We account for irrigation overspray, slope, and runoff when scoping thickness and base work.

- Cleaner access with less dust and loose gravel
- A surface that can be resurfaced later without a full tear-out
- Edges tied to the garage, sidewalk, and landscaping
- Water directed away from the foundation and the apron

## How we pave a driveway

We start with a free on-site look. You share the approximate size in square feet, and we confirm it, check truck access, and note anything the paver cannot reach. Tight side yards sometimes need a smaller machine or hand work, and that belongs in the quote.

Prep is the job. We saw-cut clean limits, haul off failed pavement, and rebuild soft spots. Aggregate goes down in lifts and is compacted before any hot-mix shows up. On pave day the asphalt is placed and rolled while it can still densify. We check the apron, the garage joint, and the way water leaves the surface.

Before we leave, you get a simple care note: when cars can return, how long to keep trailer jacks and motorcycle kickstands off the fresh mat, and when sealcoating will actually help. Fresh asphalt in St. George should cure through a season before it is sealed.
