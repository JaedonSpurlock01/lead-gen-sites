## What driveway paving covers

Asphalt driveway paving replaces gravel, broken concrete, or tired blacktop with a smooth surface you can plow, walk, and park on. Pocatello Asphalt Paving builds residential drives across Pocatello, from older grid streets near downtown to steeper lots above the valley floor.

A driveway is a small pavement with a hard job. It carries the same car in the same wheel path every day, then takes a plow blade all winter. If the base is soft or the slope traps water against the garage, the surface will crack no matter how black it looks on day one.

We price new construction, removal and replacement, and overlays. An overlay only makes sense when the existing asphalt is stable and the edges still have height against concrete, landscaping, and garage floors. If the drive has alligator cracking or pumping fines, we recommend taking it out.

Whether you call it asphalt driveway paving, blacktop driveway work, or gravel-to-asphalt conversion, the scope is the same: a compacted base and hot-mix asphalt built for Pocatello winters.

## Why homeowners pave with asphalt

A paved drive cuts dust, gives snow a place to slide off the blade, and makes the front of the house look finished. Buyers notice a failing drive before they notice a new faucet. Asphalt also lets us come back in later years and mill or overlay instead of jackhammering a full concrete slab.

Southeast Idaho adds a few rules. Meltwater refreezes in hairline cracks. Deicer and gravel tracked off the street grind the surface. Irrigation overspray on the edge of a drive keeps the soil soft under the mat. We talk through those conditions on the estimate so the thickness and the slope match the way you actually use the property.

- Smoother winter access and easier snow removal
- A surface that can be resurfaced later without a full tear-out
- Edges tied to the garage, sidewalk, and landscaping
- Water directed away from the foundation and the apron

## How we pave a driveway

We start with a free on-site look. You share the approximate size in square feet, and we confirm it, check truck access, and note anything the paver cannot reach. Tight side yards sometimes need a smaller machine or hand work, and that belongs in the quote.

Prep is the job. We saw-cut clean limits, haul off failed pavement, and rebuild soft spots. Aggregate goes down in lifts and is compacted before any hot-mix shows up. On pave day the asphalt is placed and rolled while it can still densify. We check the apron, the garage joint, and the way water leaves the surface.

Before we leave, you get a simple care note: when cars can return, how long to keep trailer jacks and motorcycle kickstands off the fresh mat, and when sealcoating will actually help. Fresh asphalt in Pocatello should cure through a season before it is sealed.
