## Parking lots that stay open for business

An asphalt parking lot is the front door of a commercial property. Customers judge the business by the first pothole they hit, and employees notice every pond that ices over in January. Pocatello Asphalt Paving builds and resurfaces lots for retail, offices, churches, apartments, and small industrial yards in Pocatello.

Lots fail in lanes, not in empty stalls. Drive aisles, dumpster pads, and the path to the street take the load. A pretty sealcoat over a broken aisle does not fix the structure. We separate cosmetic work from sections that need milling, patching, or a new base before we write the price.

You tell us the approximate size in square feet and how the lot is used. Delivery trucks, church traffic one day a week, and all-day customer parking are different designs. The estimate says which areas are full depth and which can be overlaid.

Parking lot paving and parking lot resurfacing are common searches here; we write the quote around drive aisles, drainage, and any parking lot repair the base still needs.

## What a sound lot gives you

A graded lot sheds water instead of storing it. That protects the subgrade, reduces ice, and keeps rock out of the storefront. Clean edges at sidewalks and ADA routes matter as much as the color of the mat. We plan paving around the way people actually walk from the stall to the door.

Property managers also need a schedule that respects business hours. We sequence the work so a portion of the lot can stay open when the site allows it, and we say so before the first saw cut. Night or phased work is a scope item, not a surprise.

- Drive aisles built for the vehicles that use them
- Drainage that keeps meltwater off pedestrian routes
- Patches and overlays matched to the damage, not a single blanket price
- A surface ready for striping once the mat has cured

## From failed asphalt to a paved lot

The free consultation covers square footage, inlet locations, existing cracks, and access from the public street. We look for settling at the approach, raveling in the aisles, and patches that already rock underfoot. Those clues tell us whether the base is still doing its job.

On construction day we mill or remove the failed areas, correct grade, and pave with hot-mix compacted in a continuous pass. Joints against concrete gutters and building walks are tooled so they do not become the next crack. We haul millings and debris off the site so you can restripe and reopen.

Sealcoating and crack fill come later, on a maintenance cycle, after new asphalt has cured. If the lot only needs maintenance, say that on the form and we will scope repair and sealcoating instead of a full pave. Many Pocatello lots can gain years with the right repair before a replacement is due.
