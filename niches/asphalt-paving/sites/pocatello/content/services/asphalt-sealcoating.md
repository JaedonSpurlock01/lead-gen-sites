## Sealcoating protects cured asphalt

Asphalt sealcoating is a surface treatment. It is not new pavement, and it will not fix a failed base. On cured, stable asphalt it restores a darker wearing face, fills fine texture, and slows the oxidation and water intrusion that southeast Idaho sun and snow encourage.

Pocatello Asphalt Paving sealcoats driveways and parking lots after we confirm the mat is ready. Brand-new asphalt needs time, often a full season and commonly 6 to 12 months, before sealer belongs on it. Sealing too early traps oils and tracks onto shoes, garage floors, and store tile.

Older pavement is a better candidate once cracks are filled and broken areas are patched. We will not spread sealer across alligator cracking and call it maintenance. If the surface has already failed, we talk about repair or replacement first.

Parking lot sealcoating and driveway seal coat jobs share one rule: the pavement must be cured, clean, and structurally sound before the first coat goes down.

## What sealcoating does here

High-desert sun bakes the light oils out of asphalt and leaves it gray and brittle. Winter adds water, freeze-thaw, and deicer. A sound sealcoat cycle slows that aging on pavement that still has structure. It also gives a parking lot a uniform look after patches, which matters for a storefront on a busy Pocatello street.

Sealcoating is a maintenance habit, not a one-time rescue. Most lots and drives do well on a cycle of several years, adjusted for traffic and how harsh the previous winter was. We put the recommended timing in the estimate so you can budget the next visit.

- A wearing face that slows oxidation on sound asphalt
- Crack fill and patches completed before sealer goes down
- Clean edges at concrete, garages, and landscaping
- A return-to-traffic time you can post for customers or family

## How we sealcoat a property

The free estimate starts with square footage and a look at the surface. We note oil spots, raveling, open cracks, and areas that need a patch instead of sealer. Oil spots are cleaned or primed so the coating bonds. Cracks are filled. Vegetation at the edge is pulled so it does not grow through the new face.

Application happens on a dry day with temperatures that let the coating cure. We apply an even coat, keep it off concrete that should stay bare, and block the pavement until it can take traffic without scuffing. You get a clear window for when cars, foot traffic, and striping can return.

Parking lots often need a layout conversation before anyone paints new lines. We can schedule sealcoating so striping follows after the coating has cured, rather than painting the day the sealer goes down.
